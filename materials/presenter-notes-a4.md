# Presenter notes - Leader session 4: The six domains side by side

**Length:** 45 min · **Audience:** CxO, VPs, founders · **Mode:** thinking, no code

## Run of show
- **0-3 · Welcome.** One line: "the method you learned survives any business - watch." Set the promise: same three questions, six domains.
- **3-22 · Part 1, same method, six shapes.** Open the 2x3 SVG. Walk the three shapes: multiplicative (ecom, ad, bookings), bridge (SaaS ARR), funnel (marketing, brand). Land the punchline: name the shape and you know how a drop will behave.
- **22-42 · Part 2 + 3, read across + pick one.** Walk the leading/lagging table row by row - let the room guess each leading indicator before you reveal it. Tell the boardroom-moment story (one leader, five units, three questions). Close on Lean Analytics: one top-line per unit, three to five drivers.
- **42-45 · Q&A.** Park deep domain debates; point to b4-b9 for the hands-on trees.

## Preflight
- Have a live example of the room's OWN business unit ready to slot into the table.
- Know your audience's home domain so you can lead with the shape they already feel.
- Confirm the projector-zoom toggle works on the two SVGs.

## Never cut
- The three shapes (multiplicative / bridge / funnel) - this is the whole session.
- The three questions from the boardroom moment - it is the transferable skill.
- "One top-line per unit" - the anti-sprawl discipline is why leaders trust the method.

## Cut if long
- The marketplace "two trees at once" self-study aside.
- The "one metric changes as the business matures" card - mention in one line instead.
- Reading the full leading/lagging table aloud; pick three rows.

## Likely exec questions
- **"My business is nothing like these six."** Then it is a seventh shape of the same three. Name the top line, its levers, their owners - the method holds.
- **"Isn't a bridge just subtraction, not decomposition?"** It is decomposition by flow instead of by rate. You still assign each flow an owner.
- **"How do I pick THE one metric when three feel equal?"** Pick the current constraint - the lever most limiting growth now. It is allowed to change next quarter.
- **"What if a driver genuinely has no owner?"** That is the finding. An unowned driver is an unwatched one; assigning it is the first fix.
- **"Does this replace our dashboards?"** No - it tells you which one metric on the dashboard leads the review, and demotes the rest to leaves.
