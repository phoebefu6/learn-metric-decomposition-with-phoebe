# Presenter notes - Leader session a2: Leading vs lagging

Audience: CxO, VPs, founders. 45 min, thinking-mode, no code. Room should leave able to sort any
lever into leading or lagging, and to apply the predictive-AND-controllable test.

## Run of show (45 min)
- 0-3 Welcome. Recap a1 in one line (a number is an outcome; behind it, a tree). Today: sort the
  levers by WHEN they move.
- 3-11 Part 1, live card "rear-view mirror and the road ahead". Land: lagging = outcome, late;
  leading = input, early.
- 11-15 Show the timeline SVG. Point to the 3-week gap between the leading turn (wk3) and the
  lagging turn (wk6). That gap is the whole value.
- 15-20 Part 1 self-study North-Star shape - one output, a few inputs. Keep brief.
- 20-30 Part 2, live card "steer by leading, report by lagging". Introduce the two-part test.
  The team-that-only-watched-revenue story lands here.
- 30-42 Part 3, live card "lead time, correlation, can-we-move-it". Show the checklist SVG
  (predictive / early / controllable / owned). Tell the beautiful-metric-nobody-could-touch story.
- 42-45 Quiz (3 Qs) + Q&A.

## Preflight
- Open a2, projector zoom on, test Expand all.

## Never cut
- The 3-week lead-time gap on the timeline SVG (the visual thesis).
- "Steer by leading, report by lagging. Manage the leaves, report the root."
- The predictive-AND-controllable test - both, or the metric is out.

## Cut if long

- Part 3 self-study "trap of the uncontrollable leader" - the live card already makes the point.

## Likely exec questions + crisp answers
- "Aren't lagging metrics the ones that matter to the board?" Yes - report those. But you cannot
  steer by them; by the time they move it is too late. Steer by the leading inputs.
- "How do we know a leading metric is really predictive?" Track record: when it moved before, did
  the outcome follow, repeatedly? A one-off is a coincidence, not a signal.
- "We watch a great predictor we cannot influence." That is context, not a steering metric. Put it
  next to the macro climate; do not run the team on it.
- "How many leading inputs should we track?" Three to five per output. More and you have a wall of
  tiles, not a steering wheel.
- "Isn't a leading metric just easier to game?" Only if it is controllable-but-not-predictive.
  Keep the predictive link real and Goodhart bites less (full treatment in a6).
