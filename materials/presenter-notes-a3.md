# Presenter notes - Leader session a3: Decompose and read a drop

Audience: CxO, VPs, founders. 45 min, thinking-mode, no code. Room should leave able to name a
tree's shape at a glance and run the four-question diagnosis walk from any chair.

## Run of show (45 min)
- 0-3 Welcome. Recap a1+a2 in a line. Today the mindset becomes a method: a number just fell.
- 3-11 Part 1, live card "name the shape, know the read". Walk the three-trees SVG:
  multiplicative (percent), additive (dollars), bridge (flows).
- 11-15 Tell the DuPont-was-multiplicative / ARR-is-a-bridge contrast - the same leader switches
  reading modes.
- 15-20 Part 1 self-study "real trees mix shapes" - keep brief; the payoff is "name it per branch".
- 20-30 Part 2, live card "why the order is the whole trick". Show the 4-step funnel SVG. Drill
  the order: real? branch? segment? baseline?
- 30-36 Part 2 self-study "is it real - the question everyone skips" + the baseline trap.
- 36-42 Part 3, live card "one number, three glances". Tell the five-minute-revenue-drop story -
  this is the closer, land it hard.
- 42-45 Quiz (3 Qs) + Q&A.

## Preflight
- Open a3, projector zoom on, test Expand all.
- Confirm both SVGs render: the three-trees strip and the four-step funnel.
- Have a live drop ready (ideally the audience's own) to walk the four questions against in Q&A.
- Whiteboard ready to name the shape of a volunteer's top-line tree.

## Never cut
- The three shapes and their three different reads (percent / dollars / flows).
- The four questions IN ORDER - the order is the lesson, not the list.
- The five-minute-revenue-drop story - preparation beats talent is the takeaway.

## Cut if long
- Part 3 third self-study card (resist-the-fix) - say the one line and move on.
- Part 3 self-study "cadence makes it a habit" - a5/a6 cover cadence fully.
- Trim "real trees mix shapes" to a single sentence past 24 min.

## Likely exec questions + crisp answers
- "What if the tree does not point clearly?" Then the branch split is too coarse - go one level
  deeper on the biggest mover, or check you named the shape right.
- "How do we know it is noise vs a real drop?" Is it bigger than the usual weekly wobble, and is
  the data complete? If either fails, it is not a drop yet.
- "Which baseline is right?" The one that matches the decision. Plan for accountability, last year
  for trend, last week for operations. State it out loud first.
- "My team jumps straight to fixes." That is the habit to break. The tree costs 90 seconds; a wrong
  fix costs a sprint. Hold the room on 'which branch moved?'
- "Isn't five minutes unrealistic?" Only if the tree is not already on the wall. The speed comes
  from decomposing before the crisis, not during it - that is a5 and a6.
